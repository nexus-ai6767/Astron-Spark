Summary

ASTRON Pro is a digital download delivered after manual payment verification. If something goes wrong with a purchase, we will make it right or refund you in the cases below. Free editions are free, so there is nothing to refund.

When we refund

  • Duplicate payment. You paid more than once for the same order.
  • Paid but not delivered. Your payment was verified (or should have been) and we could not unlock your download within 7 days of your request.
  • A defective download we cannot fix. The file is corrupt or does not run as described, and we could not resolve it with you within 14 days of your report.
  • Wrong amount. You were charged an amount different from the price shown.

When we do not

  • You changed your mind after a working download was delivered.
  • Your computer does not meet the published requirements, which you can check beforehand with the system check on the home page.
  • The problem is caused by third-party software such as Ollama or an AI model.

How to request one

  1. Email jyotipatil9476@gmail.com from the address you used for the order.
  2. Include your order reference and what went wrong. Screenshots help.
  3. For a refund, tell us the UPI ID to send the money to.

How long it takes

We aim to reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds are sent by UPI to the account you name, normally within 7 business days.
