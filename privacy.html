Who we are

This website is operated by [operator’s legal name and address — to be added]. In this policy, “we” means that operator. For privacy questions email jyotipatil9476@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Payment submissions (ASTRON Pro). When you submit a payment reference we store the name you enter, your email address, the payer name, and the UPI transaction reference (UTR), plus an order reference and its status.
  • Messages you send us. The custom-build form opens your own email app with the text you typed. We only receive it if you send that email.
  • Website assistant questions. A question you type into the assistant is sent to our server. If an AI provider has been configured for the assistant, the question is forwarded to that provider to produce an answer; otherwise a built-in guide answers it.
  • Technical logs. Like any web server, ours and our host’s may log your IP address, browser type and the pages requested for security and reliability.

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this site, and we do not sell personal data.

How we use it

  • To verify your payment and deliver the product you bought.
  • To answer support requests and handle refunds.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse (for example, rate-limiting requests).

Third parties

  • GitHub. Your browser requests public repository information from api.github.com to show star and download counts, and the Android APK is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address as part of those requests.
  • Payment apps and banks. Payments are made by UPI in your own payment app. We never see your bank login or card details.
  • AI provider (optional). See “Website assistant questions” above. Do not type personal or sensitive information into the assistant.

The fonts on this site are served from the site itself, not from a third-party font service.

Storage and cookies

We do not set tracking cookies. Your browser may store small preferences on your own device (language, sound on/off, assistant voice on/off). Clear your site data to remove them.

Order records are kept on our server in private storage and are retained only as long as needed for delivery, support, refunds and any legal or accounting obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, subject to legal record-keeping duties. Email us from the address you used for your order. Your rights may vary by where you live; if applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, gives you additional rights, we will honour them.

The ASTRON software itself

The ASTRON apps are designed to run on your own device with a local model. By default they do not send your conversations to us. If you connect an external service yourself, that service’s own privacy terms apply.
